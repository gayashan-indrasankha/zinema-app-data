Zinema privacy policy

Zinema is an Android app for discovering movies and TV series. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts and does not send personal information to us. The movies you save or like are stored only on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Movie lists and pictures

The app downloads the movie list and posters from GitHub Pages and plays short clips from Facebook. These services receive your IP address when the app loads files from them, like any website you visit.

WhatsApp requests

To get a movie you send a request message in our WhatsApp group. The group's members, and our bot, can see your WhatsApp name and number in the group, as in any WhatsApp group. The bot keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of your WhatsApp ID to apply fair-use limits, and the title you asked for. WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page.
